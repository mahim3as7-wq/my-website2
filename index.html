Regular health tips

Short, practical habits for students and families. New tips are added here over time.

Tip 1

Sleep enough

Most adults need 7 to 9 hours a night, and teenagers a little more. Good sleep helps memory, mood and immunity.

Tip 2

Drink water through the day

Sip regularly instead of waiting until you are thirsty, and drink more in hot weather or when you sweat.

Tip 3

Rest your eyes

Every 20 minutes of screen or book time, look at something about 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

Tip 4

Move every day

Aim for about 30 minutes of walking or light exercise on most days, and stand up between long study sessions.

Tip 5

Wash your hands

Wash with soap before eating and after using the toilet. It is one of the simplest ways to prevent infections.

Tip 6

Do not ignore warning signs

Fever that will not settle, chest pain, difficulty breathing or sudden weakness need a doctor, not a home remedy.

General information only. For any medical problem, please see a registered doctor.
